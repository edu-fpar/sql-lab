# Languages

- The interface exists in French, English and Spanish: FR / EN / ES selector at the top of the
  page, or `?lang=en` / `?lang=es` in the address.
- Exercises keep their own language. The DCG exam papers are in French; demo exercises are
  provided in English (`demo-en`) and Spanish (`demo-es`).
- The AI prompt follows the interface language: it asks for questions in English or Spanish.
- Course reminders and this documentation exist in all three languages.
